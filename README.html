About DIARIES OF GRADE 4B

DIARIES OF GRADE 4B is a school-themed book by Shreyas Madhavan. Its world centres on a class of students and the teachers who guide them. This page introduces the main cast and celebrates the everyday school experiences, friendships, lessons, and memories that make Grade 4B special.
